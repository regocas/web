## Cookie policy

### 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on the user's device when visiting this website which, among other things, make it possible to remember preferences or analyse how the site is used.

### 2. Cookies used

At present, this website is informational and does not install analytics, advertising or social media cookies. Where applicable, it may use strictly necessary technical cookies required for its operation (for example, those set by the hosting service itself), which do not require consent under Article 22.2 LSSICE. If analytics tools, social media or other non-essential cookies are added in the future, a settings panel will first be put in place allowing users to grant, refuse or customise their consent by category, and this policy will be updated with a table detailing each cookie used (name, purpose, origin and duration).

### 3. Legal basis and consent

In accordance with Article 22.2 LSSICE, installing cookies that are not strictly necessary requires the user's prior, informed and express consent, which may be given, refused or configured through the cookie settings panel available on the website.

### 4. Managing cookies

Users may change their cookie preferences at any time through the site's settings panel, and may also configure their browser to block or delete installed cookies.

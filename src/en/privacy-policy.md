## 2. Personal data processed

At present, this website is purely informational and does not include any forms that collect users' personal data. If contact, donation, volunteering or newsletter features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to set out, in accordance with Article 13 GDPR, the data processed in each case and the purpose.

## 3. Purposes of processing

Should contact, donation, volunteering or subscription forms be added to the website, the data provided would be processed in order to: (i) answer enquiries and requests for information; (ii) manage donations and the relationship with donors; (iii) send communications about the Foundation's activities, where the user has consented; (iv) manage participation as a volunteer or collaborator.

## 4. Legal basis

Any processing of data provided through forms would be based on the consent of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Independently of this, the Foundation processes certain technical browsing information (e.g. IP addresses logged by the hosting provider) on the basis of its legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), in order to ensure the security of the website and prevent fraudulent use.

## 5. Recipients and processors

Data will not be disclosed to third parties except where required by law, or to providers acting as data processors (e.g. web hosting, donation platform), with whom the data processing agreements required by Article 28 GDPR will be signed. No international transfers of data outside the European Economic Area take place, unless expressly stated.

## 6. Retention period

Data will be kept for as long as the relationship with the data subject continues and, thereafter, for the statutory limitation periods for any liabilities.

## 7. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and may withdraw their consent at any time, by writing to {{ site.legal.privacyEmail }} or by post to calle Río Ulla, nº 10, 15179 Oleiros (A Coruña), Spain, for the attention of the Secretary of the Board of Trustees. They may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

## 8. Security

The Foundation applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure the security and integrity of personal data and to prevent its alteration, loss, or unauthorised processing or access.

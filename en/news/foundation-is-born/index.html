Fundación Martina is born

We are officially introducing Fundación Martina, a non-profit organisation created to fund biomedical and genetic research into ataxias and rare diseases linked to the VPS13D gene. Based in A Coruña, Galicia, our goal is to support affected patients and families, and to drive forward lines of research that today receive scarcely any resources.

The Foundation takes its name from Martina, a ten-year-old girl living with a neurodegenerative ataxia. Her story, and that of the many families facing a similar situation, is the starting point of a project that aims to turn uncertainty into knowledge, and isolation into companionship.

Since its founding, the Foundation has set out three lines of work that sum up our purpose: researching, by funding and promoting scientific projects on ataxias and rare diseases; supporting, by accompanying patients and families with information and guidance; and transforming, by sharing science with rigour and raising public awareness of these low-prevalence conditions.

In the coming weeks we will be announcing the Foundation's first steps: the launch of the Scientific Advisory Committee, our first collaboration agreements with research centres, and the inaugural event where we will introduce the project to everyone who wants to join us.

Thank you to everyone who is already part of this journey.
